## Remaining findings, in priority order

These are source-review findings; they do not have regression coverage in this pass.

1. **P1 — Build status and baselines advance before jobs finish.** `ArchApi.place` and
   `PreviewApi` set `built = true` on submission; `arch.revise` stores its new baseline before
   the revision finishes. Cancelling a queued build, a partial failure, or a subsequent undo
   leaves registry state ahead of the world. Original terrain is also captured at submission,
   before earlier queued writes finish. Associate buildings with their jobs and history,
   capture originals when execution starts, and retain separate desired/applied state.
2. **P1 — Same-name previews overwrite the baseline of an existing building.** `ArchApi.preview`
   replaces `Entry.plan` and `Entry.compiled` while keeping `built` and originals. Confirming a
   smaller/moved replacement does not restore cells omitted from the new preview, and discarding
   it does not recover the previous built plan. Keep staged preview state separate from the
   applied building, and confirm a diff against the applied baseline.
3. **P1 — Registry persistence loses world and dimension identity.** `Services.create` uses
   `<gameDir>/mcmcp/buildings`; `BuildingRegistry.save` writes only plan JSON. Loading resets the
   dimension, build state and original terrain, while listing only shows entries loaded in this
   session. A saved Nether plan can be interpreted in the overworld after restart, and separate
   single-player saves can share names. Scope storage to the world and persist versioned
   metadata; distinguish saved designs from world-bound buildings.
4. **P1 — Component removal restores block states without block entity data.**
   `BuildingRegistry.originals` contains `BlockState`, and revision removal uses `PlaceJob`.
   Removing a component that replaced a chest/sign restores an empty block. Preserve
   `BlockSnapshot` including NBT and restore it through the job/history path.
5. **P2 — Overlapping previews can erase each other's client display.** Discard/eviction sends
   real blocks without replaying another active preview over those positions. Define a preview
   stacking policy or reject overlaps.
6. **P2 — Interior inspection uses inconsistent field of view.** `ArchApi.inspect` plans views
   with at least 85 degrees when interiors are requested, but capture still receives the original
   FOV. Align planning and rendering parameters and verify exterior/interior framing in-client.
